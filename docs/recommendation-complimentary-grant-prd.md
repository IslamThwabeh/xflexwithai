# Complimentary Recommendation Access PRD

## Context

Client Haya Iyad Odeh could not receive the Live Package copier service (الناسخ) because the service is currently facing operational issues and is producing significant losses. As compensation, support should offer access to the Recommendations service for 30 days at no charge.

## Decision

Grant the client 30 days of Recommendations access starting immediately from the support confirmation date.

For this case, the access must:

- Start today, with no protection or waiting period.
- Last for 30 calendar days from the activation date.
- Be complimentary and must not create new income, sales revenue, payment records, or financial ledger entries.
- Apply only to Recommendations access, not LexAI, Live Package access, course access, or copier service access.
- Be recorded with the reason: `Compensation for unavailable live copier service`.

## Support Team Instruction

Support should inform the client that she can use the Recommendations service for 30 days starting today.

Suggested message:

> Hi Haya, thank you for your patience. Since we were unable to provide the live copier service (الناسخ) due to the current issue, we have activated the Recommendations service for you free of charge for 30 days starting today. You can start using it immediately.

Arabic version:

> مرحباً هيا، شكراً لصبرك. بما أننا لم نتمكن من توفير خدمة الناسخ حالياً بسبب المشكلة الموجودة في الخدمة، قمنا بتفعيل خدمة التوصيات لك مجاناً لمدة 30 يوماً ابتداءً من اليوم. يمكنك استخدامها مباشرة من الآن.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The client has active Recommendations access immediately after the grant.
- The subscription is not pending activation.
- No protection period is applied.
- The end date is 30 days after the activation date.
- The grant is marked as complimentary or zero-value.
- Financial reports do not count this grant as new income.
