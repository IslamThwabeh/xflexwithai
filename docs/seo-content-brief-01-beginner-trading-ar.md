# SEO Content Brief 01 — تعلم التداول للمبتدئين

Status: draft brief; do not publish until business and subject-matter review
Source language: Arabic
English counterpart: adapt only after Arabic approval

## Page objective

Create XFlex's foundational beginner guide: a responsible, practical answer to how someone should start learning trading without profit promises or pressure to open an account immediately.

## Intended reader

An Arabic-speaking beginner in Palestine or the surrounding region who:

- is unsure whether trading is suitable for them;
- is comparing free learning, self-study, and academies;
- does not yet understand risk, brokers, platforms, or a trading plan;
- may be vulnerable to promises of fast or guaranteed profit.

## Search intent and proposed URL

- Primary intent: informational with later commercial consideration.
- Provisional primary query: `تعلم التداول للمبتدئين`.
- Supporting queries: `تعلم التداول من الصفر`, `كيف أبدأ التداول`, `دورة تداول للمبتدئين`, `أساسيات التداول`.
- Proposed Arabic slug: `/ar/articles/learn-trading-from-zero-beginners-guide`.
- Proposed English counterpart: `/en/articles/learn-trading-from-zero-beginners-guide`.

Final query selection must be validated with Search Console and keyword-planning data before publication.

### English working title

Learn Trading from Zero: A Practical Beginner Roadmap | XFlex

## Answer-first opening requirement

The first 80–120 Arabic words should directly answer the query:

- learning trading begins with understanding risk and market mechanics;
- a beginner should not start with signals or promised returns;
- use education, controlled practice, recordkeeping, and a defined loss limit before considering real capital;
- the guide is educational and not personalized investment advice.

Do not open with a sales pitch or a long academy introduction.

## Proposed structure

1. **ما هو التداول، وما الفرق بينه وبين الاستثمار؟**
   - Define both simply.
   - Explain time horizon and activity without declaring one universally better.
2. **هل التداول مناسب لكل شخص؟**
   - Risk tolerance, financial capacity, time, emotional pressure, and realistic expectations.
3. **ما الذي يجب أن يتعلمه المبتدئ أولاً؟**
   - Market basics, order types, fees/spreads, leverage, risk, technical/fundamental context, and recordkeeping.
4. **اختيار السوق والمنصة والوسيط**
   - Educational comparison criteria.
   - Direct readers to the relevant Palestinian regulator and warn against unlicensed entities.
   - XFlex must not imply that it is a broker or personalized adviser.
5. **التدريب قبل استخدام المال الحقيقي**
   - Demo practice limits, a fixed learning exercise, and why demo results do not prove future performance.
6. **إدارة رأس المال وحجم الصفقة**
   - Explain maximum planned loss and position sizing conceptually.
   - Link to the future risk-management pillar rather than overloading this guide.
7. **بناء خطة تداول وتوثيق الصفقات**
   - Entry premise, invalidation, maximum loss, exit logic, and journal review.
8. **كيف تختار دورة أو أكاديمية تداول؟**
   - Method, curriculum, risk teaching, named expertise, transparent limitations, support, and independence.
   - Link to the future academy-selection guide.
9. **أخطاء البداية الشائعة**
   - Chasing signals, leverage, copying screenshots, changing strategy constantly, and funding with essential money.
10. **خطة تعلّم مقترحة لأول 30 يوماً**
    - A conservative educational sequence, not a promise to become profitable in 30 days.
11. **أسئلة شائعة**
    - هل يمكن تعلم التداول مجاناً؟
    - كم يحتاج تعلم التداول من وقت؟
    - هل الأرباح مضمونة بعد الدورة؟
    - هل الحساب التجريبي كافٍ؟
    - ما الفرق بين الأكاديمية والوسيط؟
12. **الخطوة التالية**
    - Offer one useful free resource first.
    - Present the Basic package as an optional structured route, not the only path.

## Original XFlex evidence needed

The business owner or approved instructor should provide:

1. The exact beginner learning sequence currently taught by XFlex.
2. Three recurring beginner questions received through WhatsApp or support.
3. Two common beginner mistakes observed by the instructor, with anonymized real context.
4. One original chart or platform screenshot that can be annotated for education and legally published.
5. The instructor/reviewer name, public biography, relevant qualifications, and approval to display them.
6. The free XFlex resource that should be the article's first call to action.
7. Confirmation of the countries and regulator references relevant to XFlex clients.

Without these inputs the draft would be generic and should not be published as an expert pillar.

## Media requirements

- One original 1200×630 social image with a descriptive Arabic title.
- At least one original annotated educational visual.
- Descriptive Arabic alt text that explains the image rather than stuffing keywords.
- No profit screenshots, luxury imagery, broker affiliate promotion, or unverifiable performance graphics.

## Structured data and QA

- Use `Article` structured data that matches visible author, reviewer, dates, image, and language.
- If FAQ structured data is used, every question and answer must be fully visible on the page.
- One H1 only; descriptive H2/H3 hierarchy.
- Arabic page uses `lang="ar"` and RTL; English counterpart uses `lang="en"` and LTR.
- Verify canonical and reciprocal `hreflang` only after both language versions are ready.
- Check mobile layout, source links, internal links, social preview, sitemap inclusion, and indexable rendered HTML.

## Success measurement

After indexing, track:

- non-branded impressions and clicks for beginner-learning queries;
- country, device, and Arabic landing-page performance;
- click-through rate after sufficient impressions;
- free-content clicks, Basic-package views, WhatsApp/contact actions, and registration starts;
- assisted conversions, without attributing every later enrollment solely to this page.

Do not judge the guide by daily rank or the broad query alone.
